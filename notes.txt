the chain rule bit. if

    z = x . W + b
    a = sigmoid(z)
    E = mean((a - y)^2)

then dE/dW is dE/da * da/dz * dz/dW which is

    dE/da = 2(a - y)      the 2 is a constant, ignore it
    da/dz = a(1 - a)      sigmoid derivative out of a itself
    dz/dW = x             so x transpose dotted with the delta

for the hidden layer the delta comes from the layer above dotted with that
layers W transpose. thats the back propagation bit, the same number gets
passed backwards layer by layer.
